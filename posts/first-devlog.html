Devlog #1: What Sonance is

03.08.2026

First entry, so: what is this?

Sonance is a 2D story-driven RPG and Metroidvania fusion experience, about consciousness and what is left of a person when it fails. The game is currently in early development, with a playtesting campaign expected to begin soon.
